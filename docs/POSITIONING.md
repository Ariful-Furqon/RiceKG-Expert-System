# Scientific Positioning: Accuracy vs Explainability in RiceKG

RiceKG demonstrates that an ontology-grounded expert system matches or exceeds supervised machine learning baselines (92.50% vs 55.50%–63.75% exact-match accuracy on synthetic cases; 84.38% vs 84.38% on field cases) while completely eliminating the dependency on labelled training data through zero-shot symbolic inference. In smallholder agricultural decision support, where diagnostic errors carry direct economic and agronomic liabilities, the definitive contribution of RiceKG is not marginal empirical superiority over black-box classifiers, but rather complete knowledge transparency, deductive auditability, and confidence-graded reasoning under open-world conditions. As demonstrated across our architectural ablation study ([`results/ablation.md`](../results/ablation.md)) and comparative baselines ([`results/baselines.md`](../results/baselines.md)), multi-tier rule stratification provides domain experts with auditable derivation traces and explicit separation between pathognomonic confirmation and exploratory screening, enabling responsible field deployment while operating within the documented vocabulary coverage boundaries disclosed in [`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`](LIMITATIONS.md).
