# Scientific Positioning: Symbolic Reasoning Boundaries and Explainability in RiceKG

On the independent field benchmark (`data/benchmark_field.csv`), RiceKG correctly identifies 0 out of 5 positive disease cases (0.0% positive-case recall, micro-F1 0.00, 95% CI [0.0, 0.0]), with its 84.38% aggregate exact match resulting solely from correctly rejecting 27 negative control cases. Diagnostic efficacy on independent field cases is currently unproven due to closed-vocabulary gating, where real-world symptom nomenclature fails to map into the reasoner's 45-term ontology vocabulary.

The 92.50% exact match achieved on `benchmark_synthetic.csv` represents rule-firing verification across `rule_derived` cases constructed from the system's own Horn clauses, rather than a comparative empirical victory over supervised machine learning. Rather than claiming predictive superiority, the defensible contribution of RiceKG rests on zero-training-data symbolic inference, fully auditable deductive derivations, and confidence-graded multi-tier stratification ([`results/ablation.md`](../results/ablation.md)), providing a transparent semantic framework whose real-world utility remains contingent upon resolving the vocabulary bottleneck disclosed in [`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`](LIMITATIONS.md) and [`results/field_failure_analysis.md`](../results/field_failure_analysis.md).
