# Institutional Ethics Oversight & Human Subjects Protection

## 1. Institutional Ethics Oversight & Protocol Registration

Research involving human participants (field agricultural extension officers, plant protection specialists, and rice farmers) evaluating the RiceKG expert system was designed and conducted in accordance with national and institutional ethical standards for empirical socio-technical research:

- **Institutional Oversight**: Lembaga Penelitian dan Pengabdian kepada Masyarakat (LP2M) / Komisi Etik Penelitian (Research Ethics Commission), **Universitas Jember (UNEJ)**, Jember, Jawa Timur, Indonesia.
- **Protocol Classification**: Exempt / Minimal Risk Research involving educational decision support, non-invasive digital interface evaluations, and professional task performance without physiological or psychological distress.
- **Regulatory Framework**:
  - Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (*Undang-Undang Perlindungan Data Pribadi / UU PDP*).
  - Indonesian Ministry of Agriculture Guidelines on Extension Knowledge Transfer (*Peraturan Menteri Pertanian tentang Penyuluhan Pertanian*).
  - WMA Declaration of Helsinki (Ethical Principles for Medical and Behavioral Research Involving Human Subjects).

---

## 2. Informed Consent Protocol

Prior to participating in any evaluation session (usability, explainability, or adoption trials), all participants are provided with a written **Participant Information Sheet and Informed Consent Form** available in both Bahasa Indonesia and English.

Key elements guaranteed to each participant:
1. **Voluntary Participation**: Participation is entirely voluntary. Participants may decline to answer any question or terminate the session at any point without penalty, loss of compensation, or effect on their professional relationship with Dinas Pertanian or Universitas Jember.
2. **Explicit Study Objectives**: Participants are informed that the study evaluates the software's explanation traces and decision support interfaces, **not their personal competence or professional expertise**.
3. **No Direct Agronomic Liability**: Decisions made during experimental sessions are simulated against historical scouting scenarios and do not affect active farm fields or chemical spray schedules.
4. **Debriefing**: Following the session, participants receive full disclosure regarding which cases were concordant, near-miss, or negative controls, accompanied by verified phytopathological reference diagnoses.

---

## 3. Data Confidentiality & Anonymity Procedures

In compliance with UU No. 27/2022 (PDP Law):
- **De-Identification**: All survey responses, audio transcripts, and task logs are linked only to synthetic alphanumeric participant IDs (e.g., `P01`, `P02`, ...). Master keys linking names and contact numbers are stored on an encrypted drive accessible solely to the Principal Investigator.
- **Demographic Aggregation**: Professional roles and experience are reported solely in aggregate (e.g., "Extension officers, mean experience 8.4 years"). No individual extension post or specific farmer group will be identifiable in published manuscripts.
- **Open Science Data Sharing**: Empirical data files deposited in public repositories (`studies/*/responses.csv`) contain solely de-identified quantitative survey ratings and task metrics. No personally identifiable information (PII) is included.

---

## 4. Conflict of Interest & Commercial Impartiality

- The development of RiceKG and associated evaluation studies are funded exclusively through academic research grants.
- The authors declare no financial or commercial relationships with pesticide, agrochemical, or seed supply corporations.
- All IPM prescriptions generated by the system recommend cultural and biological control methods first, adhering to FAO and Indonesian Ministry of Agriculture Integrated Pest Management principles.
