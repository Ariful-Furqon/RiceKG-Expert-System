About RiceKG Framework

An Explainable Semantic Knowledge Graph and Multi-Tier SWRL Reasoning System for Rice Biotic Stressor Diagnosis and Prescriptive Management.

System Conceptualization

Department of Information Technology, Faculty of Computer Science, Universitas Jember

RiceKG addresses the fundamental limitations of modern black-box machine learning models and brittle production-rule expert systems by integrating formal OWL 2 Description Logic (DL) conceptual ontologies with Semantic Web Rule Language (SWRL) inference powered by the Pellet Reasoner.

The framework formalizes 10 major biotic threats (5 destructive insect pests and 5 prevalent phytopathogenic diseases), 45 hierarchical phenotypic symptoms categorized by anatomical organ, and 30 actionable Integrated Pest Management (IPM) treatments.

OWL 2 Domain Ontology

Rigorous TBox and ABox formalization capturing biological taxonomies, organ relations, and phenological growth stages.

Multi-Tier SWRL Rules

Tier 1 canonical rules, Tier 2 relaxed rules under partial symptom observations, and Tier 3 multi-threat co-infection rules.

100% Explainable XAI

Zero black-box opacity. Every diagnosis and prescriptive recommendation is backed by a mathematical First-Order logic proof trace.

Pellet Reasoner

Java-based Tableau DL reasoner executing dynamic forward-chaining inference with thread-safe entity lifecycle management.

Open-Source & Reproducibility

To foster open smart agriculture research, the complete ontology (rice_ontology.owl), SWRL rule bases, benchmark datasets (dataText.csv), and web application are freely available on GitHub.
